These terms govern your purchase of services from EaseMe (contact@easeme.live). By purchasing any service from us, you agree to these terms and conditions.

Our Services

We provide NHS job application support services including personal statement writing, application form completion, application submission, and interview preparation. Full details of each service are described on our website at easeme.live.

Payment

Full payment is required at the time of purchase. All prices are stated in GBP. We do not guarantee interviews, sponsorship, or employment outcomes. These depend solely on the employing NHS organisation.

Interview Guarantee

Where a package includes a guaranteed number of interviews, this applies subject to you providing accurate information about your qualifications, experience, and right to work. The guarantee covers confirmed interview invitations only and does not guarantee a job offer, as hiring decisions are made solely by the employing NHS organisation.

No Transfer of Access

Your package is personal to you. If you are offered a role before your package is fully used, your remaining access ends and cannot be transferred to another person. A new candidate requires a new purchase.
